Unless applicable law permits the use independently, you may not without prior written permission:

- use Threadlight as the name of a modified version, hosted service, company, domain, or product;

- remove a clear distinction between the official project and a modified distribution.

Modified distributions should use a distinct name and clearly state that they are not the official Threadlight project. They may truthfully describe themselves as forks of or based on Threadlight.

To request permission, contact the maintainer through the [official repository](https://github.com/Lecarvalho/threadlight).
